# NewEngineH3VR status

Updated during implementation on 23 October 2026. **The indoor range, wrist menu and quickbelt checkpoint has passed a physical playtest, and the user also played Take and Hold successfully.** Earlier checks confirmed good visuals, corrected controller alignment, spawning a 1911 and shooting a target, audible gunshots and vibration. Full gameplay parity is not claimed. See [HEADSET_PLAYTEST.md](HEADSET_PLAYTEST.md) and [PLAYER_SYSTEMS.md](PLAYER_SYSTEMS.md) for acceptance records and [MVP.md](MVP.md) for launch instructions. The original Steam files remain untouched. No Wine process is used by the native probes or build pipeline.

The current package is `dist/NewEngineH3VR.app`: 25 ARM64 binaries, 126 managed
assemblies and seven OptiH3VR optimization targets. Scoped scene/CPU fixtures,
cached and uncached graphics checks, and a bounded profiling/restoration check
passed. See [OPTIH3VR.md](OPTIH3VR.md) for the current integration and evidence.
The earlier physical acceptance remains scoped to its recorded builds; this
optimization checkpoint still needs its own physical retest and establishes no
overall FPS gain. Historical component counts and hashes below describe their
named checkpoints.
